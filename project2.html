Streamlit Dashboard & Recommendation for Students Bringings Cars to Campus

Purpose and Context

  • Project Purpose: Provide a Python-driven Streamlit Dashboard that informs transfering or prospective students about whether they should bring their car to campus using data analytics.
  • Implementation Details: The dashboard will integrate multiple data sources (campus parking data, student survey data, Google Maps API data) to provide visualizations and insights on commute times, parking availability, costs, and environmental impact.
  • Data Details: Data will be sourced from fictional campus parking datasets, student survey results, and real-time data from the Google Maps API regarding commute times for various transportation methods.
  • Desired Outcome: A user-friendly dashboard that helps students make informed decisions about bringing their cars to campus, along with a final report summarizing findings and recommendations.
  • Additional Context: The project aims to address common concerns among students regarding transportation options, costs, and environmental impact. By leveraging data analytics and visualization, the team seeks to empower students with actionable insights.
  • Glossary of Terms:
    • Streamlit: An open-source Python library used for building interactive web applications and dashboards.
    • Google Maps API: A service that provides geographical data and mapping functionalities, including commute times and routes.
    • Data Cleaning: The process of preparing and correcting data to ensure accuracy and consistency for analysis.
